About

What I actually do

A wind turbine is a power station that has to be serviced 80 metres in the air, in whatever weather the site gives you. The work is part mechanical, part electrical, and mostly diagnostic: something has stopped, and the machine is telling you about it in alarm codes and trend curves rather than words.

My background is mechanical — I started in 2013 erecting Enercon steel-concrete hybrid towers — and the electrical side came from the years after that, on service crews where you are expected to do both. Gearbox, converter, yaw and pitch fault finding; blade inspections; torque and tensioning work; commissioning and the reports that go with it.

I am based in Izmir and currently work the Aliaga and Bergama wind farms for Bilgin Enerji. I have also worked project sites across Europe with international crews, which is where most of my English comes from.
