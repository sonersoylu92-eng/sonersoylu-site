Who is Soner?

Soner SoyluTechnique learned on site.

How I work

Observe, measure, understand, test, solve, learn

Every step is grounded in a real case from my field notes; follow the link to read the whole case.

  1. Observe

    I try to recognise a fault by its sound, vibration and heat before the alarm code tells me.
    Yaw bearing wear caught with no alarm →

  2. Measure

    When the control system reading and a physical measurement disagree, I verify the sensor first.
    Generator temperature alarm →

  3. Understand

    With an intermittent fault I look for the condition it appears in, not the fault itself.
    Converter overcurrent →

  4. Test

    I look at the data before deciding on a part: the vibration spectrum before a blade inspection, a physical measurement of the pitch angle.
    Blade vibration →

  5. Solve

    I try to fix the problem without stopping production where possible; if needed the turbine keeps running at reduced power until the part arrives.
    Producing at a 2.2 MW limit →

  6. Learn

    I take a lesson from every case and share it as a field note. In the pitch motor case the current rise was visible in the SCADA trend 8 months earlier.
    Pitch motor high current →
