Training · fault diagnosis

When the turbine stops, thinking starts

Scenarios built from real field cases. Read the alarm and choose the next step yourself. After every choice you see why it was right or premature, what that measurement tells you, and what was actually found on site.

Educational simulation · not a substitute for OEM procedures or site safety rules

  1. Turbine
  2. Alarm
  3. Data
  4. Observation
  5. Hypothesis

The scenarios come from real cases in the field notes; the findings are the values measured in those cases. On every intervention your own turbine OEM instructions, LOTO procedure and site safety rules govern; work near energised equipment is done only by authorised personnel.

For technical background on each system, see the systems pages; for terms, the glossary.
