Glossary

Wind turbine terms

On site the talking happens in one language and the manual is written in another. This glossary sits in that gap: every term here I have used at the foot of a tower or inside a nacelle, with the Turkish equivalent next to it. The definitions come from the work, not from a dictionary.

148 termsEnglish – Turkish6 categoriesUpdated September 2026

148 terms shown

A

Airfoil

Kanat profili

The cross-sectional shape of the blade. Wind turbines use thick sections from the NACA or DU families.

Aerodynamics

Anchor cage

Ankraj kafesi

The steel stud cage cast into the foundation that the first tower section bolts down to.

Components

Anchor point

Ankraj noktası

A certified fixed point that safety equipment may be attached to.

Safety

Anemometer

Anemometre

The wind speed sensor on top of the nacelle. The controller decides cut-in and cut-out from it.

Components

Angle of attack

Hücum açısı

The angle between the relative wind and the blade chord. Too large and the flow separates: the blade stalls.

Aerodynamics

Automatic lubrication unit

Yağlama ünitesi

The automatic greaser that pumps measured grease into bearings and gear teeth on a schedule.

Components

Availability

Erişilebilirlik

The share of time the turbine is available to produce. Contracts often penalise anything under 97 percent.

Aerodynamics

B

Betz limit

Betz sınırı

The theoretical maximum a rotor can extract: 59.3 percent, or 16/27. It cannot be beaten.

Aerodynamics

Blade

Kanat

An aerodynamic shell of glass or carbon fibre composite. On an N90 it is 43.8 m long and weighs six to seven tonnes on its own.

Components

Blade bearing / Pitch bearing

Pitch yatağı

The toothed slewing bearing between blade root and hub that lets the blade rotate.

Components

Blade root

Kanat kökü

The cylindrical end of the blade that bolts to the hub, held by hundreds of studs.

Components

Blade tip

Kanat ucu

The outermost end of the blade. It sees the highest speed and the worst erosion.

Components

Bolt tensioning

Gerdirme

Stretching the bolt directly with a hydraulic tensioner and seating the nut. Preferred on large flanges.

Maintenance

Borescope inspection

Endoskopi

Inspecting inside the gearbox with a camera without opening it. The fastest way to see tooth surface damage.

Maintenance

Brake pad

Fren balatası

The friction element in the brake caliper. Measuring pad wear is a standard item in every scheduled service.

Components

C

Capacity factor

Kapasite faktörü

Annual energy produced divided by what the machine would make running flat out all year. Typical onshore values in Türkiye are 30 to 38 percent.

Aerodynamics

Chord

Veter uzunluğu

The distance from leading edge to trailing edge of a blade section. It tapers from root to tip.

Aerodynamics

Circuit breaker

Kesici

The switching device that interrupts fault current. Vacuum types are common at medium voltage.

Electrical

Commissioning

Devreye alma

The process of testing a newly built turbine and bringing it into production.

Maintenance

Condition monitoring system (CMS)

Durum izleme sistemi

Vibration sensors permanently installed in the nacelle for continuous monitoring.

Maintenance

Cone angle

Koni açısı

The slight forward opening of the blades out of the rotor plane, serving the same purpose.

Aerodynamics

Confined space

Kapalı alan

A space with restricted entry and poor ventilation. The hub and the inside of a blade both count.

Safety

Contactor

Kontaktör

An electromagnetically operated power switch, used in motor control.

Electrical

Converter

Konvertör

The power electronics that match generator output to grid voltage and frequency.

Components

Cooling unit

Soğutma ünitesi

The radiator and fan set that cools gearbox oil and the generator. One of the biggest sources of summer alarms.

Components

Corrective maintenance

Düzeltici bakım

Repair after a failure. It is unplanned and the downtime cost is high.

Maintenance

Coupling

Kaplin

The flexible connection between gearbox and generator. It absorbs misalignment and vibration, and slips under overload.

Components

Curtailment

Güç kısıtlama

Deliberately reducing output because of the grid, noise or shadow limits. It is not a fault.

Control

Cut-in wind speed

Devreye girme hızı

The lowest wind speed at which the turbine starts producing, typically 3 to 4 m/s.

Aerodynamics

Cut-out wind speed

Devreden çıkma hızı

The upper limit where the turbine shuts down for safety, usually 25 m/s.

Aerodynamics

D

Delamination

Delaminasyon

Composite layers separating from each other. It heads the list of structural blade damage.

Maintenance

DFIG

Çift beslemeli asenkron jeneratör

A generator whose rotor winding is fed through a converter. It makes variable speed economical; this is what the N90 uses.

Electrical

Direct drive

Direkt tahrik

A design where the rotor turns the generator without a gearbox. Enercon's signature.

Electrical

Disc brake

Disk fren

The mechanical brake on the high-speed shaft. It backs up aerodynamic braking and holds the rotor for parking and service.

Components

Downtime

Duruş süresi

Time the turbine cannot produce. In contracts it has a price.

Maintenance

E

Earthing / grounding

Topraklama

Bonding equipment to earth potential. The basis of both lightning and touch safety.

Electrical

Emergency stop (E-stop)

Acil durdurma

A stop triggered by a red button, breaking the safety chain directly. The blades go to feather.

Control

Encoder

Enkoder

A sensor measuring angle or speed. Pitch and yaw position are read from it.

Electrical

F

Fall arrester

Düşme durdurucu

The device that locks onto the ladder rail or rope and stops a fall.

Safety

Fault code / alarm code

Alarm kodu

The number the control system raises for a specific fault. Most service work follows that number.

Control

Feathering / Feather position

Bayrak konumu

Turning the blade roughly 90 degrees so it offers minimum resistance. It is the primary way to stop a turbine.

Aerodynamics

Flange

Flanş

The ring-shaped face where two sections meet and are bolted together. The most critical place for torque checks.

Components

Foundation

Temel

The reinforced concrete mass that carries the turbine. A machine in the N90 class sits on 500 to 800 m³ of concrete.

Components

Frequency converter (VFD)

Frekans çevirici

A drive that sets motor speed by changing frequency, used on yaw and pitch motors.

Electrical

Fretting corrosion

Fretting korozyonu

Wear and corrosion at tightly fitted surfaces caused by micro-movement.

Maintenance

Full body harness

Tam vücut emniyet kemeri

The wearer's side of the fall arrest system. It is inspected by eye before every use.

Safety

Full power converter

Tam güç konvertörü

A converter the whole generator output passes through. It gives the most flexible grid compliance.

Electrical

G

Gearbox

Dişli kutusu

The transmission that steps the rotor's roughly 15 rpm up to the 1,500 rpm the generator needs.

Components

Generator

Jeneratör

The machine that turns mechanical rotation into electricity. The N90 uses a doubly-fed induction type.

Components

Global Wind Organisation

GWO

The body that sets the industry's common training standard. Its certificates are effectively mandatory for site access.

Safety

Grease

Gres

The thickened oil used in bearings and gear teeth. The wrong type will finish a bearing.

Maintenance

Grid code

Şebeke kodu

The rules a plant must follow to connect to and behave on the grid. They differ by country.

Electrical

H

Harmonics

Harmonik

Distorting current and voltage components at multiples of the fundamental frequency, produced by power electronics.

Electrical

Helical stage

Helisel kademe

The parallel-shaft stages after the planetary stage. These do most of the speed increase.

Components

High-speed shaft (HSS)

Yüksek hız mili

The shaft between gearbox output and generator. The mechanical disc brake usually sits on it.

Components

HMI

HMI

The screen through which an operator talks to the turbine, on the panel at the tower base.

Control

Hub

Göbek

The cast structure the blades bolt onto, carried by the main shaft. It houses the pitch drives and their backup batteries.

Components

Hydraulic power unit (HPU)

Hidrolik ünite

The pump and accumulator set that supplies pressure to brakes, pitch or locking systems.

Components

I

Icing

Buzlanma

Ice forming on the blade. It causes imbalance, lost output and an ice-throw risk, and calls for a shutdown.

Aerodynamics

Induction / asynchronous generator

Asenkron jeneratör

A generator whose rotor turns with slip relative to grid frequency. Simple and rugged.

Electrical

L

Leading edge

Hücum kenarı

The rounded edge that meets the wind first. Rain and dust erosion starts here.

Components

Leading edge erosion

Hücum kenarı erozyonu

Wear of the blade's leading edge from rain and dust. It costs production and is repaired with coating.

Maintenance

Lightning protection system (LPS)

Yıldırım koruma sistemi

The chain of receptor, conductor and earthing. One of the most frequently measured systems on site.

Electrical

Lightning receptor

Yıldırım alıcısı

The metal disc at the blade tip. It catches the strike and takes it down a conductor to earth.

Components

Limit switch

Sınır anahtarı

A switch detecting a mechanical end position. It stops pitch and yaw from over-travelling.

Electrical

Lock-out tag-out (LOTO)

Etiketleme-kilitleme

Cutting the energy and securing it with a physical lock and tag. The first step of any electrical work.

Safety

Low voltage ride through (LVRT)

Düşük gerilim atlatma

The turbine's ability to stay connected through a short grid voltage dip. It is a grid code requirement.

Electrical

Low-speed shaft (LSS)

Düşük hız mili

The shaft between hub and gearbox, turning at rotor speed.

Components

M

Main bearing

Ana yatak

The large bearing supporting the main shaft. Replacing it is one of the most expensive failures in the machine.

Components

Main shaft

Ana şaft

The shaft that carries the slow rotation from the hub into the gearbox.

Components

Major component exchange (MCE)

Büyük bileşen değişimi

Replacing a heavy part such as a gearbox, generator or blade using a crane. A costly operation lasting days.

Maintenance

Medium voltage (MV)

Orta gerilim

Generally the 6 to 36 kV range. The turbine transformer output and site distribution sit here.

Electrical

Met mast

Meteoroloji direği

An independent measurement mast on site. It is the reference for power curve verification.

Components

N

Nacelle

Nasel

The machine house on top of the tower. It holds the main bearing, gearbox, brake, generator and control cabinet.

Components

O

Oil analysis

Yağ analizi

Testing a gear oil sample for metal particles and chemical degradation. It warns early.

Maintenance

P

Permanent magnet generator (PMG)

Sabit mıknatıslı jeneratör

A generator with magnets in the rotor, needing no excitation current. Common on direct drive machines.

Electrical

Permit to work

İş izni

The system that makes sure risky work is done under a written permit and defined conditions.

Safety

Pitch control

Pitch kontrolü

Setting power and loads by rotating the blade about its axis. The standard on modern machines.

Aerodynamics

Pitch drive

Pitch motoru

The electric (or hydraulic) drive that turns the blade. On loss of supply, batteries drive it to feather.

Components

Pitch system

Pitch sistemi

The system that rotates each blade about its own axis to set the angle of attack and therefore the power.

Components

Pitting

Çukurlaşma

Small pits forming on a bearing or tooth surface. The early stage of fatigue damage.

Maintenance

Planetary stage

Planet kademesi

The high-torque input stage of the gearbox, which spreads load across several planet gears.

Components

PLC

PLC

The turbine's main control computer. It reads every sensor, drives every actuator and raises the alarms.

Control

Power coefficient (Cp)

Güç katsayısı

How much of the wind's energy the rotor actually takes. A good machine peaks around 0.45 to 0.50.

Aerodynamics

Power curve

Güç eğrisi

The curve of power against wind speed. It is the turbine's identity card.

Aerodynamics

Power factor (cos φ)

Güç faktörü

The ratio of active to apparent power. The grid operator usually dictates a band for it.

Electrical

PPE

Kişisel koruyucu donanım

Helmet, glasses, gloves, steel-toed boots, hearing protection and harness.

Safety

Prebend

Prebend

The slight upwind curvature built into the blade. It keeps the tip clear of the tower under load.

Aerodynamics

Predictive maintenance

Kestirimci bakım

Catching a failure early from vibration, oil and temperature data and acting before it happens.

Maintenance

Preload

Ön yükleme

The tensile force in the bolt produced by tightening. Preload is what actually holds the joint, not the torque itself.

Maintenance

Proximity sensor

Yakınlık sensörü

A non-contact position detector, used for rotor lock, hatches and limit sensing.

Electrical

PT100 (RTD)

PT100

A sensor measuring temperature through resistance change. Standard for bearing and winding temperatures.

Electrical

R

Rated power

Anma gücü

The maximum power the turbine can hold continuously. 2,500 kW for the N90.

Aerodynamics

Rated wind speed

Anma rüzgâr hızı

The wind speed at which rated power is first reached. Above it, pitch holds power constant.

Aerodynamics

Reactive power

Reaktif güç

The non-working component of power exchanged to support grid voltage, measured in kVAr.

Electrical

Relay

Röle

A signal-level switching and protection device.

Electrical

Remote reset

Uzaktan reset

A reset done from SCADA without going to site. Some alarms are locked out of it.

Control

Rescue kit

Kurtarma seti

The rope system used to lower an injured worker down the tower. Every team must carry one.

Safety

Reset

Reset

Clearing the alarm and returning the turbine to production. A reset without a cause found will repeat.

Control

Risk assessment

Risk değerlendirmesi

Identifying hazards and controls in writing before work starts.

Safety

Root cause analysis (RCA)

Kök neden analizi

The work of finding the real cause of a failure. The only way to stop it repeating.

Maintenance

Rotor

Rotor

The rotating assembly of blades, hub and spinner. This is what converts the wind's kinetic energy into rotation.

Components

Rotor lock

Rotor kilidi

The pin that mechanically stops the rotor turning. It goes in before anyone enters the hub.

Safety

S

Safety chain

Güvenlik zinciri

The hardwired circuit with every critical safety contact in series. If any one opens, the turbine stops.

Control

SCADA

SCADA

The system for monitoring and commanding every turbine on site remotely. Production, alarm and trend data live here.

Control

Scheduled / preventive maintenance

Periyodik bakım

Planned maintenance on a calendar, usually packaged into six and twelve month services.

Maintenance

Serrated trailing edge

Testere firar kenarı

A serrated add-on fitted to the trailing edge. It lowers aerodynamic noise.

Aerodynamics

Service crane

Servis vinci

The small crane inside the nacelle. Good for tools and parts, not for major components.

Components

Service lift

Servis asansörü

The single-person lift inside the tower. The ladder still counts as the escape route.

Components

Service mode

Bakım modu

The operating mode a turbine is put in for maintenance. Remote commands are disabled.

Control

Setpoint

Set değeri

The target value a control loop is trying to reach.

Control

Shadow flicker

Gölge titreşimi

The strobing shadow cast by a turning blade. It is a planning constraint near housing.

Aerodynamics

Shear web

Kesme ağı

The vertical internal wall joining the two spar caps. It carries the shear load.

Components

Slip ring

Slip ring

The brush and ring assembly that carries power and signals between the rotating hub and the fixed nacelle. Carbon dust build-up is a classic fault.

Components

Sound power level

Ses gücü seviyesi

The total acoustic power the turbine emits, in dB(A). It can be turned down in a night mode.

Aerodynamics

Spar cap

Kiriş / spar

The main composite band inside the blade that carries the bending load. The spine of the blade.

Components

Spinner / Nose cone

Burun konisi

The aerodynamic cover over the hub. Everyone calls it the spinner; you climb through it to service the pitch system.

Components

Stall

Stall

Flow separating from the blade when the angle of attack gets too large. Lift drops, drag rises.

Aerodynamics

Swept area

Süpürme alanı

The circle the rotor sweeps. Power scales directly with it: 6,362 m² on an N90.

Aerodynamics

Switchgear

Şalt hücresi

The cabinet holding the medium voltage breaker, disconnector and instrument transformers, at the tower base.

Components

T

Thermal imaging

Termal kamera

Finding loose connections and hot spots in cabinets from a heat image.

Maintenance

Thermistor

Termistör

A temperature protection element embedded in a winding. It stops the motor on overheating.

Electrical

Tightening torque

Sıkma torku

Tightening a bolt to a specified value. The manufacturer's figure always governs.

Maintenance

Tilt angle

Tilt açısı

The angle of the main shaft to horizontal, about 5 degrees. It increases blade-to-tower clearance.

Aerodynamics

Tip speed

Kanat ucu hızı

The linear speed of the blade tip. Onshore it is usually capped near 80 m/s because of noise.

Aerodynamics

Tip speed ratio (λ)

Kanat ucu hız oranı

The ratio of blade tip speed to wind speed. Modern machines run best between 7 and 9.

Aerodynamics

Toolbox talk

Saha başı toplantısı

The short safety briefing held before starting work, covering the day's risks.

Safety

Tower

Kule

The tapered steel shell that lifts the rotor to where the wind is strong and steady. Usually erected in three to five sections.

Components

Tower section

Kule segmenti

One piece of the tower, bolted to the next through a flange. Transport limits decide how long a section can be.

Components

Trailing edge

Firar kenarı

The sharp rear edge of the blade. Most of the aerodynamic noise comes off it.

Components

Transformer

Trafo

The transformer that steps generator voltage up to medium voltage. It sits at the tower base or inside the nacelle.

Components

Trend log

Trend kaydı

The time-stamped record of measurements. Root cause usually shows up in the trends.

Control

Turbulence intensity (TI)

Türbülans yoğunluğu

The variation in wind speed relative to the mean. High turbulence drives fatigue loads and failures.

Aerodynamics

Twist

Burulma

The twist built into the blade from root to tip. It keeps the angle of attack right at every station.

Aerodynamics

U

UPS

Kesintisiz güç kaynağı

The battery-backed supply that keeps the control system alive when mains is lost.

Electrical

V

Vibration analysis

Titreşim analizi

Finding bearing and gear damage from the vibration spectrum.

Maintenance

Vortex generator

Vorteks jeneratörü

Small fins bonded onto the blade. They delay flow separation and can add a few percent of output.

Aerodynamics

W

Wake effect

İz etkisi

The slowing and added turbulence downwind of a turbine. It cuts the output of machines behind it.

Aerodynamics

Weibull distribution

Weibull dağılımı

The statistical model describing how wind speeds are distributed at a site, defined by shape k and scale c.

Aerodynamics

Wind rose

Rüzgâr gülü

The polar chart of how wind is distributed by direction. Turbine layout is planned from it.

Aerodynamics

Wind shear

Rüzgâr kayması

The increase of wind speed with height. It means the upper blade sees faster wind than the lower one.

Aerodynamics

Wind vane

Rüzgâr yön ölçer

The vane that measures wind direction. It is the reference for the yaw system.

Components

Working at Heights (WAH)

Yükseklikte çalışma

One of the GWO basic modules, covering tower climbing, equipment use and rescue.

Safety

Y

Yaw bearing

Yaw yatağı

The large toothed slewing bearing between nacelle and tower. Its teeth are grease lubricated.

Components

Yaw brake

Yaw freni

The calipers that hold the nacelle once it reaches its target heading. They apply partial pressure while yawing.

Components

Yaw drive

Yaw motoru

The geared electric motor that produces yaw movement. A turbine may carry four to eight of them.

Components

Yaw misalignment

Yaw sapması

The nacelle sitting off the true wind direction. Even a few degrees costs measurable production.

Aerodynamics

Yaw system

Yaw sistemi

The system that turns the nacelle into the wind: motors, ring gear, yaw bearing and brakes.

Frequently asked

About these terms

What is a nacelle?
The machine house on top of the tower, holding the main bearing, gearbox, brake, generator and control cabinet. It sits on the yaw bearing and turns so the rotor faces the wind.
Pitch or yaw — what is the difference?
Pitch is the blade turning about its own axis, which sets the angle of attack and therefore power and load. Yaw is the whole nacelle turning on the tower to face the wind. One turns the blade, the other turns the machine.
Why is this glossary bilingual?
Turbine manufacturers are mostly European, so manuals and alarm lists arrive in English while the crew speaks the local language. Anyone working on turbines outside the manufacturer's home country lives in both vocabularies at once.
Something looks wrong or missing.
Then write to me. This came out of one technician's experience; other manufacturers and other sites use words differently. If you know a better equivalent, send it with the source and I will add it.
