What this assistant does, and what it does not

It is not a search engine and not a chatbot. It finds the sections of this site that bear on your question and answers from those alone. Underneath the answer you get the pages it came from, so you can open the original article and read the full context.

That limit is deliberate. In turbine work an invented torque figure or temperature threshold turns into a real risk on the tower. So the assistant is not allowed to speak from general knowledge: it would rather say it could not find something than say something the site does not actually state.

Warning: what you read here is field experience and does not replace the manufacturer's service documentation. On every intervention your own turbine's OEM instructions, LOTO procedure and site safety rules govern. If in doubt, do not start it.

If it cannot find an answer

The assistant cannot answer on a subject the site does not cover. When that happens, send the question to me directly from the contact section. Questions that keep coming back get written up and added to the site permanently — which means what you asked becomes something the assistant will know next time.
