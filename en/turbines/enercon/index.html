Manufacturer

Enercon Series

Enercon's design signature is that it uses no gearbox. The rotor drives an annular generator directly. That removes the single largest failure category in the industry; in exchange it brings a very heavy generator and a sensitivity to thermal management.

The field consequence of the gearless design is plain: there is no oil change, no gear pitting and no reduction-stage failure on the maintenance list. What replaces them is the generator cooling ducts — a blocked duct comes straight back as a temperature alarm.

2.3 MWEnercon E7070 m rotor, direct-drive annular generator, no gearbox. Cooling-duct cleaning is the main periodic job. 2.0–3.0 MWEnercon E8282 m rotor, direct-drive. E2 and E4 variants, storm control, annular generator maintenance. 2.35 MWEnercon E9292 m rotor, low-wind design. Large rotor with deliberately low rated power; yaw bearing monitoring comes to the front.
