## 2) YouTube · İngilizce (K-044)

**Title**

```
World's Tallest Wind Turbine (1,197 ft): A Technician's First Question #Shorts
```

**Description**

```
The world's tallest wind turbine, in Germany, has a 126 m rotor. So does the Vestas V126 I service in the field. The difference is the tower.

- Schipkau, Germany: hub at 300 m (984 ft), blade tip at 365 m (1,197 ft).
- The V126 hub sits between 87 and 166 m, depending on version.
- The developer expects about twice the energy of a turbine with the same rotor size. That is a projection, not yet proven in operation.
- No crane today can lift the nacelle that high. The fix is a telescoping tower: the inner tower is lowered for maintenance, then raised again.

The tower changes, the rule doesn't: never climb a tower alone. Always at least two.

Full V126 specs: https://sonersoylu.com/en/turbines/vestas/v126/

Sources: Electrek (Oct 2, 2026), Wikipedia "Schipkau GICON Wind Turbine".
```
