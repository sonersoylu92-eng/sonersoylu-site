## İngilizce sürüm (K-044) · anlatım

1. The world's tallest wind turbine is 1,197 feet. If its generator failed at 984 feet, how would you get it down?
2. Schipkau, Germany. Hub at 300 meters, blade tip at 365. Taller than the Eiffel Tower.
3. Here's what caught my eye: the rotor is 126 meters across. Same as the Vestas V126 I service. But the V126 hub sits between 87 and 166 meters, depending on version.
4. Why so tall? Winds higher up are stronger and more consistent. The developer expects about twice the energy of a turbine with the same rotor. That's still a projection.
5. My first question as a technician is different: maintenance. No crane today can lift the nacelle that high.
6. The fix: a telescoping tower. The inner tower slides down inside the lattice. The crane reaches the machine. Then it goes back up.
7. The tower changes. The rule doesn't: never climb a tower alone. Always at least two.
8. Full V126 specs at sonersoylu.com/en. The Turbine Tech. Follow for the next question.
